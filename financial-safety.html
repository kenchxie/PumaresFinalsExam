Financial Safety

Practical information to help you make informed financial decisions.

📘

Spot warning signs

Be cautious of unexpected messages asking for passwords, bank details, or verification codes.

  • Do not share one-time passwords.
  • Check website addresses before signing in.
  • Be suspicious of urgent prize or payment claims.
📘

Secure your accounts

Use strong, unique passwords and enable additional account security when available.

  • Keep devices and apps updated.
  • Sign out on shared devices.
  • Contact your financial provider through its official channels if something seems wrong.
💡 MoneyWise reminder

Choose realistic steps, keep learning, and ask a trusted qualified person when you need help with an important financial decision.
