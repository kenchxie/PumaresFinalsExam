Needs vs. Wants

Practical information to help you make informed financial decisions.

📘

Know the difference

Needs are things required for daily living, such as food, basic clothing, housing, and transportation to school or work. Wants are things that add comfort or enjoyment but are not essential.

  • List your essential expenses first.
  • Pause before buying non-essential items.
  • Remember that priorities can change.
📘

Try a spending check

Before purchasing, ask whether the item is necessary now, whether you already have something similar, and whether it fits your plan.

  • Compare prices and options.
  • Wait before making an unplanned purchase.
  • Keep room in your budget for important needs.
💡 MoneyWise reminder

Choose realistic steps, keep learning, and ask a trusted qualified person when you need help with an important financial decision.
