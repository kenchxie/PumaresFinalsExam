Savings Goals

Practical information to help you make informed financial decisions.

📘

Make your goal specific

Decide what you are saving for, how much it may cost, and when you hope to reach it.

  • Write down the target amount.
  • Choose a realistic timeline.
  • Break the target into smaller milestones.
📘

Track your progress

Review your savings regularly and adjust the amount or timeline when needed.

  • Record each contribution.
  • Keep savings somewhere appropriate and secure.
  • Recognize steady progress.
💡 MoneyWise reminder

Choose realistic steps, keep learning, and ask a trusted qualified person when you need help with an important financial decision.
